# Running — Tempo & Threshold (Z3–Z4)

Tempo running develops lactate threshold and improves running economy. These sessions are comfortably hard — you can speak short phrases but not hold a conversation. Target %LTHR rather than pace, since terrain, heat, and fatigue all affect pace.

---

### Tempo Run 2×15 min

**Energy system**: Z4 — Threshold
**Sport**: Running
**When to use**: Build phase, 1× per week, readiness score 4–5
**Duration**: 50 min
**TSS estimate**: 65–80

**Structure**:
- 10 min easy warmup @ Z1–Z2
- 15 min @ Z4 (93–99 % LTHR)
- 5 min easy recovery @ Z1
- 15 min @ Z4
- 5 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 93–99 % LTHR — the highest sustainable running intensity
**Notes**: If HR drifts above 100 % LTHR, slow down. Cardiac drift is normal in the final minutes.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 2×12 min with 5 min recovery
- Harder: 2×20 min, or add a third 10-min block

---

### Cruise Intervals 4×8 min

**Energy system**: Z4 — Threshold
**Sport**: Running
**When to use**: Build and peak phases, alternative to longer tempo blocks
**Duration**: 55 min
**TSS estimate**: 65–80

**Structure**:
- 10 min easy warmup @ Z1–Z2
- 8 min @ Z4 (93–99 % LTHR)
- 3 min recovery @ Z1 (keep moving)
- Repeat 4 times
- 9 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 93–99 % LTHR — consistent across all 4 reps
**Notes**: The shorter reps allow slightly higher intensity and more total time at threshold than a continuous tempo block.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 3×8 min
- Harder: 5×8 min, or extend to 4×10 min
