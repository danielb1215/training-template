# Running — Intervals (Z5)

High-intensity running intervals develop VO2max, improve running economy, and build speed. These are the hardest running sessions — use sparingly and only when fully recovered.

**Use**: 1× per week in build/peak, readiness score ≥ 4.

---

### Track Intervals 6×800 m

**Energy system**: Z5 — VO2max
**Sport**: Running
**When to use**: Build and peak phases, high-quality speed work
**Duration**: 55 min
**TSS estimate**: 80–95

**Structure**:
- 15 min easy warmup @ Z1–Z2 (include 4× strides at the end)
- 800 m @ Z5 (100–106 % LTHR)
- 400 m recovery jog @ Z1 (≈ 3 min)
- Repeat 6 times
- 10 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 100–106 % LTHR — very hard but controlled form
**Notes**: Run the first rep conservatively; the last rep should be your hardest. If HR doesn't reach Z5 by rep 2, run harder.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 4×800 m
- Harder: 8×800 m, or switch to 6×1000 m

---

### Hill Repeats 8×90s

**Energy system**: Z5/Z6 — VO2max / Anaerobic
**Sport**: Running
**When to use**: Build phase, develops power and running economy, lower impact than flat intervals
**Duration**: 50 min
**TSS estimate**: 70–85

**Structure**:
- 15 min easy warmup @ Z1–Z2
- 90 s uphill hard effort @ Z5 (100–106 % LTHR, ~ 6–8 % gradient)
- Jog/walk down the hill for recovery (≈ 2–3 min)
- Repeat 8 times
- 10 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 100–106 % LTHR during efforts — feel like a sustained sprint
**Notes**: Hills reduce impact forces compared to flat intervals, making them ideal for injury-prone runners. Focus on strong arm drive and high knee lift.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 6 repeats
- Harder: 10–12 repeats, or extend to 2-min efforts
