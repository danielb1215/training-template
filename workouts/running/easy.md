# Running — Easy / Aerobic (Z1–Z2)

Easy running builds the aerobic foundation, enhances fat metabolism, and promotes recovery between hard sessions. Most of your weekly running volume should be here. If in doubt, go easier.

**HR targets use %LTHR.** Claude reads your LTHR from `athlete/profile.md`.

---

### Easy Recovery Run

**Energy system**: Z1 — Active Recovery
**Sport**: Running
**When to use**: Day after a hard session, readiness score 1–2, or on easy days in any phase
**Duration**: 30 min
**TSS estimate**: 20–30

**Structure**:
- 30 min continuous @ Z1 (< 75 % LTHR)

**Targets**: < 75 % LTHR — should feel almost embarrassingly slow
**Notes**: Walk breaks are fine. The goal is blood flow and recovery, not fitness.

**Scaling**:
- Shorter: 20 min if very fatigued
- Longer: Not applicable — if you feel good, do a different workout

---

### Aerobic Base Run

**Energy system**: Z2 — Aerobic Endurance
**Sport**: Running
**When to use**: Easy run days, base phase, 3–4× per week in high-volume training
**Duration**: 45 min
**TSS estimate**: 40–55

**Structure**:
- 5 min easy walk/jog warmup
- 35 min @ Z2 (76–85 % LTHR)
- 5 min cooldown walk

**Targets**: 76–85 % LTHR — conversational pace, could hold for hours
**Notes**: Nasal breathing is a good Z2 indicator — if you must breathe through your mouth, slow down.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: Stay at 76–80 % LTHR, include walk breaks
- Harder: Extend to 60 min, upper Z2 (82–85 % LTHR)

---

### Long Easy Run

**Energy system**: Z2 — Aerobic Endurance
**Sport**: Running
**When to use**: Weekend long run, base and build phases, readiness score 3–5
**Duration**: 75 min
**TSS estimate**: 75–95

**Structure**:
- 10 min easy warmup (Z1–Z2)
- 55 min @ Z2 (76–85 % LTHR)
- 10 min cooldown (Z1)

**Targets**: 76–85 % LTHR — pace will naturally slow on hills; don't fight it
**Fuelling**: Carry water for runs > 60 min; consider gels if > 75 min

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 60 min total
- Harder: 90–100 min, add 2× 5-min strides at the end at Z3 pace
