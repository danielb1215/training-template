# Cycling — Threshold (Z4)

Threshold work targets your FTP directly. Sessions at 88–105 % FTP improve lactate clearance, increase mitochondrial density, and raise the ceiling of your sustainable power output. These are your primary "quality" sessions in build and peak phases.

**Use**: 1–2× per week maximum. Always at least one easy day before and after.

---

### Threshold 3×10 min

**Energy system**: Z4 — Threshold
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Early build phase, first threshold session of a block, readiness score 4+
**Duration**: 60 min
**TSS estimate**: 65–75

**Structure**:
- 15 min warmup @ Z1–Z2 (include 2× 30-s builds to Z4 at min 12 and 14)
- 10 min @ threshold (88–95 % FTP)
- 5 min recovery @ Z1
- 10 min @ threshold
- 5 min recovery @ Z1
- 10 min @ threshold
- 10 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 88–95 % FTP — maximal sustainable, 1-word answers only
**Cadence**: 85–95 rpm

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 3×8 min with 4 min recovery
- Harder: Extend to 3×12 min (see next template)

### Threshold 3×12 min

**Energy system**: Z4 — Threshold
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Mid-build phase, bread-and-butter threshold session
**Duration**: 70 min
**TSS estimate**: 78–90

**Structure**:
- 15 min warmup @ Z1–Z2 (2× 30-s builds at min 12 and 14)
- 12 min @ threshold (88–95 % FTP)
- 4 min recovery @ Z1
- 12 min @ threshold
- 4 min recovery @ Z1
- 12 min @ threshold
- 11 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 88–95 % FTP
**Notes**: The classic threshold session. If you can complete all 3 intervals, your FTP assessment may be conservative.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 3×10 min
- Harder: 4×10 min, or 2×20 min (see next template)

### Threshold 2×20 min

**Energy system**: Z4 — Threshold
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Late build / peak phase, readiness score 5
**Duration**: 75 min
**TSS estimate**: 90–105

**Structure**:
- 15 min warmup @ Z1–Z2
- 20 min @ threshold (88–95 % FTP)
- 5 min recovery @ Z1
- 20 min @ threshold
- 15 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 88–95 % FTP — two 20-min efforts is the gold standard threshold session
**Warning**: Only attempt on fresh legs. Not appropriate if readiness score < 4.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 2×16 min
- Harder: 3×15 min, or push toward 95–100 % FTP

### Over-Under 3×12 min

**Energy system**: Z4/Z5 — Threshold with anaerobic surges
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Build and peak phases, teaches lactate tolerance / clearance
**Duration**: 75 min
**TSS estimate**: 90–105

**Structure**:
- 15 min warmup @ Z1–Z2
- 12 min over-under block (3 min @ 95 % FTP → 1 min @ 108 % FTP, repeat 3×)
- 4 min recovery @ Z1
- 12 min over-under block
- 4 min recovery @ Z1
- 12 min over-under block
- 12 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: "Under" = 92–95 % FTP · "Over" = 105–110 % FTP
**Notes**: The over-minute spikes clear lactate from the "under" accumulation — brutal but highly effective.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 2× blocks only, or reduce "over" to 103 % FTP
- Harder: Extend over-minute to 90 s
