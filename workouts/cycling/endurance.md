# Cycling — Endurance (Z1–Z2)

Use these sessions to build aerobic base, promote fat adaptation, and accumulate volume without creating significant fatigue. The majority of your weekly training time should be here.

---

### Recovery Spin

**Energy system**: Z1 — Active Recovery
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Day after a hard session, readiness score 1–2, or any day needing movement without stress
**Duration**: 30–45 min
**TSS estimate**: 20–30

**Structure**:
- 30–45 min continuous @ Z1

**Targets**: 45–55 % FTP — legs should feel spinning freely, zero effort
**Cadence**: 90–100 rpm recommended (promotes recovery without muscular load)

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 25 min if very fatigued
- Harder: Not applicable — if you feel good, do a different workout

---

### Easy Endurance

**Energy system**: Z2 — Aerobic Endurance
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Base and build phases, easy days, readiness score 3–5
**Duration**: 60 min
**TSS estimate**: 45–55

**Structure**:
- 10 min warmup @ Z1 (< 55 % FTP)
- 40 min @ Z2 (56–75 % FTP)
- 10 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 56–75 % FTP — conversational pace, nose breathing possible

**Scaling**:
- Easier: Stay in lower half of Z2 (56–65 % FTP)
- Harder: Extend to 75 min, stay at upper Z2 (70–75 % FTP)

---

### Aerobic Base

**Energy system**: Z2 — Aerobic Endurance
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Primary base-building session, 1–2× per week in base phase
**Duration**: 90 min
**TSS estimate**: 75–90

**Structure**:
- 15 min warmup @ Z1
- 60 min @ Z2 (56–75 % FTP)
- 15 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 56–75 % FTP — hold steady, no spikes
**Notes**: The bulk of aerobic adaptation happens here. Resist the urge to push harder.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 60 min total, more Z1
- Harder: 2 h total, last 30 min at upper Z2 (70–75 % FTP)

---

### Long Ride

**Energy system**: Z2 — Aerobic Endurance
**Sport**: Cycling (outdoor preferred)
**When to use**: Weekend long ride, base and build phases, readiness score 3–5
**Duration**: 150 min (2h 30 min)
**TSS estimate**: 130–160

**Structure**:
- 20 min warmup @ Z1
- 110 min @ Z2 (56–75 % FTP)
- 20 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 56–75 % FTP — fuelling mandatory every 45 min
**Nutrition**: 60–90 g carbs/hour during the ride

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 2 h total, stay at lower Z2
- Harder: 3 h+, include 2× 20-min blocks at upper Z2 in the final hour
