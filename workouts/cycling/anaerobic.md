# Cycling — Anaerobic (Z6)

Anaerobic efforts at 121–150 % FTP last 30 seconds to 3 minutes and develop your capacity to tolerate and clear lactate rapidly. Essential for criteriums, punchy climbs, and race attacks.

**Use**: 1× per week in build/peak, only when readiness score ≥ 4. Requires 48 h recovery.

---

### Anaerobic 6×1 min

**Energy system**: Z6 — Anaerobic Capacity
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Build and peak phases, preparing for punchy / criterium-style racing
**Duration**: 55 min
**TSS estimate**: 75–90

**Structure**:
- 15 min warmup @ Z1–Z2 (include 3× 10-s sprints at min 12, 13, 14)
- 1 min @ Z6 (130–150 % FTP)
- 4 min recovery @ Z1
- Repeat 6 times
- 10 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 130–150 % FTP — as hard as you can sustain for 60 s
**Cadence**: High — 100–110 rpm to recruit fast-twitch fibres

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 4×1 min
- Harder: 8×1 min, or extend to 90 s efforts

### Tabata 8×20s/10s

**Energy system**: Z6/Z7 — Anaerobic / Neuromuscular
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Peak phase, develop anaerobic power in time-efficient format
**Duration**: 40 min
**TSS estimate**: 60–75

**Structure**:
- 15 min warmup @ Z1–Z2 (with 3× sprint primes)
- Tabata block: 8× (20 s all-out @ > 150 % FTP / 10 s complete rest)
- 5 min recovery @ Z1
- Tabata block: 8× (20 s all-out / 10 s rest)
- 10 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: > 150 % FTP during efforts — truly maximal
**Notes**: The 10 s rest is too short for metabolic recovery; power will drop across the block — that's expected. Sprint as hard as possible every effort.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 1 Tabata block only
- Harder: 3 Tabata blocks with 5 min between
