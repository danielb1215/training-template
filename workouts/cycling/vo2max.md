# Cycling — VO2max (Z5)

VO2max intervals push your aerobic ceiling. Short, very hard efforts at 106–120 % FTP force your cardiovascular system to operate at maximum oxygen uptake. These are the highest-quality sessions in the build and peak phases and require significant recovery (48–72 h after).

**Use**: 1× per week maximum. Only when readiness score ≥ 4.

---

### VO2max 5×4 min

**Energy system**: Z5 — VO2max
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Build phase, key quality session, readiness score 4–5
**Duration**: 65 min
**TSS estimate**: 90–105

**Structure**:
- 15 min warmup @ Z1–Z2 (include 3× 20-s builds at min 10, 12, 14)
- 4 min @ Z5 (106–120 % FTP)
- 4 min recovery @ Z1 (≤ 55 % FTP — full recovery, not partial)
- Repeat 5 times
- 10 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 106–120 % FTP — sustainable for the full 4 min but not beyond
**Notes**: The recovery must be genuinely easy. If you're cutting recovery short, the quality degrades.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 4×4 min, or reduce to 108–112 % FTP
- Harder: 6×4 min, or extend to 5×5 min with 5 min recovery

### VO2max 8×3 min

**Energy system**: Z5 — VO2max
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Peak phase, shorter efforts for sharper stimulus
**Duration**: 65 min
**TSS estimate**: 88–100

**Structure**:
- 15 min warmup @ Z1–Z2 (3× 20-s builds)
- 3 min @ Z5 (110–120 % FTP)
- 3 min recovery @ Z1
- Repeat 8 times
- 10 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 110–120 % FTP — higher intensity for shorter time
**Notes**: 3-min efforts allow you to push harder than 4-min; still requires the same recovery care.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 6×3 min
- Harder: 10×3 min

---

### Micro-intervals 10×40s/20s

**Energy system**: Z5/Z6 — VO2max / Anaerobic
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Variation on VO2max, good for athletes who struggle with longer Z5 efforts
**Duration**: 60 min
**TSS estimate**: 75–90

**Structure**:
- 15 min warmup @ Z1–Z2
- 10 rounds of: 40 s @ 130 % FTP / 20 s @ Z1 (total 10 min)
- 5 min recovery @ Z1
- 10 rounds of: 40 s @ 130 % FTP / 20 s @ Z1 (total 10 min)
- 10 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 125–135 % FTP during efforts
**Notes**: The short recovery keeps VO2 elevated across the block, similar stimulus to longer intervals.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 8 rounds per block
- Harder: 3 blocks of 10 rounds (15 min total effort time)
