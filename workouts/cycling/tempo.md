# Cycling — Tempo & Sweet Spot (Z3)

Tempo and sweet-spot work sits between endurance and threshold. It builds aerobic power efficiently but creates more fatigue than Z2. Use sparingly — maximum 2× per week in build phase, rarely in base phase.

**Sweet Spot** (88–93 % FTP) is a sub-zone straddling Z3/Z4 that provides a high training stimulus per time unit — popular for time-crunched athletes.

---

### Tempo 2×20 min

**Energy system**: Z3 — Tempo
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Late base / early build, readiness score 4–5, not day before a key session
**Duration**: 60 min
**TSS estimate**: 65–75

**Structure**:
- 10 min warmup @ Z1–Z2
- 20 min @ Z3 (76–87 % FTP)
- 5 min recovery @ Z1
- 20 min @ Z3 (76–87 % FTP)
- 5 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 76–87 % FTP — breathing elevated, short sentences possible

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 2×15 min with 5 min recovery
- Harder: 3×15 min, or push toward sweet-spot range (88–93 % FTP)

---

### Sweet Spot 3×15 min

**Energy system**: Z3/Z4 — Sweet Spot
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Build phase, readiness score 4–5, 1–2× per week
**Duration**: 75 min
**TSS estimate**: 80–95

**Structure**:
- 15 min warmup @ Z1–Z2
- 15 min @ sweet spot (88–93 % FTP)
- 5 min recovery @ Z1
- 15 min @ sweet spot
- 5 min recovery @ Z1
- 15 min @ sweet spot
- 10 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 88–93 % FTP — hard but sustainable, 1–2 word answers, can hold for 20–30 min

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 3×12 min, reduce to 85–90 % FTP
- Harder: 3×20 min at 88–93 % FTP (see next template)

---

### Sweet Spot Long 2×30 min

**Energy system**: Z3/Z4 — Sweet Spot
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Build phase, once per week, readiness score 4–5
**Duration**: 90 min
**TSS estimate**: 100–115

**Structure**:
- 15 min warmup @ Z1–Z2
- 30 min @ sweet spot (88–93 % FTP)
- 5 min recovery @ Z1
- 30 min @ sweet spot
- 10 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: 88–93 % FTP — the longest sustainable sweet-spot effort
**Notes**: Elite training stimulus for FTP development. Don't do this more than once per week.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 2×20 min
- Harder: Add a third 20-min block, or increase to lower threshold (93–95 % FTP)
