# Cycling — Neuromuscular / Sprint (Z7)

Sprint sessions develop peak power output, fast-twitch muscle recruitment, and bike-handling skills. Efforts are 10–30 seconds at maximum intensity (> 150 % FTP). Full recovery between efforts is essential — these are quality, not volume.

**Use**: 1× per week, any training phase, even base. Low training stress, high neuromuscular stimulus.

---

### Standing Sprints 8×15s

**Energy system**: Z7 — Neuromuscular / Sprint
**Sport**: Cycling (outdoor preferred for safety)
**When to use**: Any phase, any day the plan allows a brief hard stimulus without deep fatigue
**Duration**: 55 min
**TSS estimate**: 40–55

**Structure**:
- 20 min warmup @ Z1–Z2 (easy spin, get warm)
- 15 s standing sprint (> 150 % FTP, all-out from seated to standing)
- 4 min 45 s recovery @ Z1 (full 5 min between starts)
- Repeat 8 times
- 15 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: > 150 % FTP — absolute maximum effort for each sprint
**Cadence**: 50–60 rpm at sprint initiation (force-based)
**Notes**: Standing start engages glutes and full-body recruitment. Each sprint must be maximal — if you're pacing, you're doing it wrong.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 5 sprints
- Harder: 10 sprints, or extend to 20 s efforts

---

### Seated Sprints 10×10s

**Energy system**: Z7 — Neuromuscular / Sprint
**Sport**: Cycling / VirtualRide
**When to use**: Any phase, also works indoors on a trainer
**Duration**: 50 min
**TSS estimate**: 35–45

**Structure**:
- 20 min warmup @ Z1–Z2
- 10 s seated sprint (> 150 % FTP at high cadence)
- 4 min 50 s recovery @ Z1
- Repeat 10 times
- 15 min cooldown @ Z1

**Targets**: > 150 % FTP — high-cadence power (100–120 rpm at peak)
**Notes**: Seated sprints emphasise fast-twitch recruitment at speed rather than raw force. Good for criterium field-sprint speed.

**Scaling**:
- Easier: 6 sprints
- Harder: Add a second block of 10 sprints after 10 min recovery
