## Why

- Heavy strength (low reps, high load) + endurance → better cycling economy and **power late in long rides** (durability), with little weight gain (Rønnestad et al.).
- Core / upper body / neck → tolerate long hours on the bike.
- Bone density (cycling alone is low-impact).

## Schedule

| Phase | Dates | Sessions/week | Days | Load |
|-------|-------|---------------|------|------|
| Adaptation | | 2 | after the ride | 3 × 10 @ moderate (RPE 6–7), learn technique |
| Max strength | | 2 (1 in deload weeks) | after the ride | 4 × 4–6 heavy (RPE 8), explosive up, 2–3 s down |
| Maintenance | | 1 | | 3 × 4–5 heavy on main lifts only |
| Stop | ~10 days before A-race | 0 | — | Mobility only |

Rules: never before key intervals or a long ride; ≥ 48 h before the long ride; expect soreness in weeks 1–2 → reduce ride intensity, not ride volume.

## Habit plan

- Anchor it to a ride: go to the gym straight after the ride.
- Minimum version = 25 min and it counts: squat/leg press 3×10 · Romanian deadlift 3×10 · step-up 2×10/leg · plank 2×45 s.
- Never miss twice.

## Session (~45 min)

1. Warm-up 8–10 min (bike/rope + hip/glute activation)
2. **Back squat or leg press** — main lift
3. **Romanian deadlift** or hip thrust
4. **Bulgarian split squat / step-up** (each leg)
5. Calf raise 3 × 12
6. Upper: **pull-up or row** 3 × 8, **push-up** 3 × 10–15
7. Core circuit ×2: plank 45 s · side plank 30 s/side · dead bug 10/side · Pallof press 10/side

## Mobility / yoga

- 15–20 min on a rest day + 5–10 min after long rides: hip flexors, hamstrings, glutes, thoracic spine, neck.

## Breathing

- **Inspiratory muscle training (IMT)**: 30 breaths × 2/day at ~50 % max inspiratory pressure, 6+ weeks.

## Supplements (evidence-based, after bloods)

| Item | Use | Note |
|------|-----|------|
| Iron | Only if ferritin low | Check Hb, ferritin (more relevant at altitude / high volume) |
| Vitamin D | Only if low | Same blood panel |
| Caffeine | 3 mg/kg before the hardest part of a race; practise in long rides | Avoid late in the day (sleep) |
| Protein | 1.6–2.0 g/kg/day; 20–30 g + carbs within 30 min after long rides | Food first |
| Beetroot juice (nitrate) | Optional, ~5 days pre-race | Test GI first |
| Creatine | Off-season option | ≈ +1 kg water |

## Sleep

Target 7.5–8.5 h; sleep is the highest-return "complement".
