## 1. How much, when

| Ride | Carbs/hour | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| < 75 min easy | 0–30 g | Water + electrolytes is enough |
| 75 min – 2 h, Z2 | 30–60 g | Use these rides for gut training |
| 2–3 h, or with tempo/threshold | 60–80 g | Glucose + fructose mix |
| > 2.5–3 h, hard, race simulation | 80–90 g | Only after gut training; 1:0.8 glucose:fructose |

- Start eating at 15–20 min, then every 15–20 min (15–25 g each time).
- Fluids: 500–750 ml/h, ~500–700 mg sodium per litre. Weigh before/after rides to learn sweat rate; lose < 2 % body weight.
- Above ~60 g/h you need glucose + fructose (table sugar and honey are already ~1:1).
