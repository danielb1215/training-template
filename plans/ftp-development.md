# FTP Development Framework

**Model**: Option 1+2 Hybrid — Polarized VO2max blocks + Sweet Spot/Threshold Build
**Athlete**: {{ATHLETE_FULL_NAME}} | FTP baseline: see `athlete/profile.md`
**Target**: Increase FTP via systematic aerobic base expansion, then progressive threshold stress

---

## Framework Overview

```
Phase 1 (Weeks 1–6):  Sweet Spot Base     → Build aerobic engine, handle volume
Phase 2 (Weeks 7–12): Threshold Build     → Push threshold ceiling higher
Phase 3 (Weeks 13–16): VO2max Sharpening → Convert aerobic base to peak FTP
Phase 4 (Week 17):    FTP Test + Deload   → Measure, reset, plan next cycle
```

Each full cycle = ~17 weeks. Repeat with updated FTP.

---

## Phase 1: Sweet Spot Base (Weeks 1–6)

**Goal**: Accumulate time at 88–93% FTP to build sustainable aerobic capacity without the recovery debt of full threshold work.

**Weekly structure** (12h/week, Tue/Thu/Sat/Sun):

| Day | Session | Duration | Intensity |
|-----|---------|----------|-----------|
| Tue | Sweet spot intervals | 75 min | 2–3 × 15 min @ 88–93% FTP, 5 min rest |
| Thu | Z2 endurance | 60 min | 56–75% FTP, steady |
| Sat | Long Z2 ride | 150 min | 56–75% FTP, optional 2 × 10 min sweet spot at end |
| Sun | Easy spin | 45 min | Z1, <55% FTP |

**Week-by-week progression**:

| Week | Tue (Sweet Spot) | Sat (Long ride) | Key focus |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------|
| 1 | 2 × 15 min | 120 min, no SS | Establish rhythm |
| 2 | 2 × 18 min | 135 min, no SS | Add volume |
| 3 | 3 × 15 min | 150 min + 1 × 10 min SS | First 3-set week |
| 4 | **Deload** — 2 × 12 min | 90 min, no SS | Absorb adaptation |
| 5 | 3 × 18 min | 150 min + 2 × 10 min SS | Volume peak |
| 6 | 2 × 20 min | 150 min + 1 × 15 min SS | Extend interval duration |

**TSS targets**: Week 1 ≈ 420 TSS → Week 6 ≈ 520 TSS (max 10% increase week-on-week)

## Phase 2: Threshold Build (Weeks 7–12)

**Goal**: Shift from sweet spot to true threshold (95–100% FTP). Increase time-at-threshold each week. This is where FTP ceiling rises.

**Weekly structure**:

| Day | Session | Duration | Intensity |
|-----|---------|----------|-----------|
| Tue | Threshold intervals | 75 min | See progression below |
| Thu | Z2 endurance | 75 min | 56–75% FTP |
| Sat | Long Z2 + threshold | 150 min | 90 min Z2 + 2 × 10 min @ 95% FTP |
| Sun | Easy spin | 45 min | Z1 |

**Week-by-week progression**:

| Week | Tue (Threshold) | Sat threshold block | Total threshold min/week |
|------|----------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| 7 | 3 × 8 min @ 95% FTP | 2 × 8 min | 40 min |
| 8 | 3 × 10 min @ 95% FTP | 2 × 10 min | 50 min |
| 9 | 2 × 20 min @ 95% FTP | 2 × 10 min | 60 min |
| 10 | **Deload** — 2 × 10 min @ 90% FTP | 1 × 10 min | 30 min |
| 11 | 3 × 12 min @ 97% FTP | 2 × 12 min | 60 min |
| 12 | 2 × 20 min @ 98% FTP | 1 × 20 min | 60 min |

**Success signal for Phase 2**: If Week 12 intervals feel sustainable (HR stabilizes, no significant power fade), FTP has likely moved.

---

## Phase 3: VO2max Sharpening (Weeks 13–16)

**Goal**: 4-min efforts at 110–120% FTP recruit fast-twitch fibers and expand VO2max ceiling — which pulls FTP up with it (Seiler polarized model).

**Weekly structure**:

| Day | Session | Duration | Intensity |
|-----|---------|----------|-----------|
| Tue | VO2max intervals | 70 min | See progression below |
| Thu | Z2 endurance | 60 min | 56–75% FTP — protect recovery |
| Sat | Long Z2 | 120 min | Purely Z2, no hard efforts — absorb VO2max stress |
| Sun | Easy spin | 30 min | Z1 only |

**Note**: Volume drops ~15% in this phase to allow VO2max quality. Do not add extra work.

**Week-by-week progression**:

| Week | VO2max Session | Details |
|------|---------------|---------|
| 13 | 4 × 4 min @ 110% FTP | 4 min recovery between efforts |
| 14 | 5 × 4 min @ 112% FTP | 4 min recovery |
| 15 | 6 × 4 min @ 115% FTP | 3 min recovery — shorter rest increases demand |
| 16 | **Sharpen** — 4 × 4 min @ 115% FTP | 4 min recovery, feel sharp not crushed |

**Execution cues**:
- Start each interval at target power. Do not go harder early.
- If you can't hold target power in the last 30 sec, reduce next week's target by 3–5%
- HR should reach 92–98% max HR by the 3rd interval

## FTP Test Protocol

### When to Test

- **Before starting**: Establish baseline before Week 1
- **After Phase 1** (end of Week 6): Optional check — use RPE sense, not mandatory
- **After Phase 2** (end of Week 12): Mandatory test
- **After Phase 3** (Week 17): Final test to capture VO2max gains

**Never test when**: TSB (form) < -20 (deeply fatigued), or within 5 days of a hard block

### Recommended: Ramp Test Protocol

Best for repeatability and recovery. Less dependent on pacing skill than 20-min test.

```
Warm-up (15 min):
  - 5 min easy Z1 spin
  - 3 × 30 sec at 100% FTP with 1 min easy between
  - 5 min easy

Ramp:
  - Start at 50% FTP
  - Increase by 5% FTP every 1 minute
  - Continue until failure (can't complete the full minute)

Calculation:
  FTP_new = max_1min_power × 0.75
```

**After test**: 2 easy Z1 days minimum before resuming training.

### Alternative: 20-Minute Test Protocol

Use if ramp test is unavailable or for comparison baseline.

```
Warm-up (20 min):
  - 10 min easy spin
  - 5 min at 75% FTP
  - 3 × 1 min all-out sprint, 1 min easy between
  - 5 min easy

Test:
  - 20 min maximal effort — start conservatively (first 5 min should feel "hard but holdable")
  - Target even power or slight negative split (slightly faster second half)

Calculation:
  FTP_new = avg_20min_power × 0.95

Altitude note: if you test at altitude, the result already reflects altitude-adjusted FTP.
If ever tested at sea level, scale down by 8% to get altitude FTP.
```

## Progression Model

### When to Advance Between Phases

**Phase 1 → Phase 2** (after Week 6): Advance if ALL of:
- Completed ≥ 4 of 6 weeks with target power held (< 5% power fade in intervals)
- Aerobic decoupling on long rides ≤ 6% (altitude-adjusted threshold)
- No more than 1 week skipped due to illness/fatigue

**Phase 2 → Phase 3** (after Week 12): Advance if ALL of:
- Week 11–12 threshold intervals feel "hard but controlled" (not survival mode)
- HR at threshold stabilizes within first 5 min of intervals (not still rising at min 15)
- Readiness scores averaging ≥ 3.5 over the last 3 weeks

**If criteria not met**: Repeat the last 2 weeks of the phase before advancing.

---

### Load Progression Rules

Applies within any phase:

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| TSS compliance ≥ 90% for 2 consecutive weeks | Advance to next week's load |
| TSS compliance 70–90% | Hold current week's load |
| TSS compliance < 70% or readiness avg < 3 | Drop back 1 week, investigate cause |
| 3+ consecutive red readiness days | Unplanned deload week immediately |

**10% ramp rule**: No single week should exceed the previous week's actual TSS by more than 10%. Always check `log/compliance.md` before planning.

---

### FTP Adjustment Rules

After a test shows FTP increase:

1. Update `athlete/profile.md` with new FTP and date
2. **Do not restart the cycle from Week 1** — return to whichever phase you're in
3. All %FTP-based targets auto-adjust via MCP `lowPct`/`highPct` — no manual recalculation needed
4. If FTP increased > 5%: consider adding 1 extra deload week before resuming hard training

**Expected FTP gains** (realistic at 2,600m altitude):
- After Phase 1 (6 weeks sweet spot): 0–3% gain (base-building, not FTP-focused)
- After Phase 2 (12 weeks total): 3–7% gain expected
- After Phase 3 (16 weeks total): 5–10% total gain from baseline

Example: at FTP 240 W, a 10 % gain = 264 W — a meaningful upgrade.

## Warning Signs — When to Back Off

Flag any of the following to Claude for immediate plan review:

- **Resting HR** up > 5 bpm above your personal baseline for 3+ days
- **HRV** (if tracked) dropping > 10% from rolling average
- **Sleep quality** self-reported ≤ 2 for 3+ consecutive days
- **Readiness score** averaging < 3 for 2+ weeks
- **Power output in intervals** declining despite adequate warm-up (not just one bad day)
- **Mood / motivation** consistently low (chronic training monotony — may need block restructure)

Any 2 of the above together = automatic deload week.
